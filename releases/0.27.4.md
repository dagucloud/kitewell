Kitewell 0.27.4 is a beta update, with the Dagu 2.27.0 engine.

## What's in this release

- Each project chooses the model its chat answers with, at the top of its
  Agents & models, where Kitewell (included) is one of the choices. A new
  project's chat answers with Kitewell's included model until you choose
  another for it.
- Choose another model, in the chat, opens the project's Agents & models.
- Why a chat turn ended, its details, and the buttons that lead on stand
  apart from one another.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell updates itself to 0.27.4 at a quiet moment, and
running workflows go on across the restart. A chat model set up for the
whole computer becomes each of its projects' own, with its key; a project
made later answers with Kitewell's included model until you choose another
for it. From 0.27.1 or earlier, it brings 0.27.2's changes as well.
