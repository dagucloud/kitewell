Kitewell 0.25.2 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- The ready-made first requests are gone from setup, the empty overview and
  the empty chat. Each needed a website, a sign-in, a spreadsheet or a
  mailbox you had not named, so none could run as offered. Kitewell now asks
  for the chore in your own words only.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.25.1 updates itself to 0.25.2 at a quiet moment,
and running workflows go on across the restart.
