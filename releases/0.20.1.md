Kitewell 0.20.1 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- Desktop steps leave Kitewell's own windows alone. A run started while the
  Kitewell window was in front maximized that window as if it were the app
  the workflow works in, and a step could have clicked in it. Kitewell now
  tells its engine that its windows are never to be resized, brought
  forward, or acted on.
- The assistant keeps answering in the language you write in during a long
  conversation. Once its own notes and long work pushed your last message
  out of what it reads, it could answer in English.
- The assistant and MCP clients now read a long workflow's whole definition,
  page by page. The end of a long workflow's YAML used to be cut off, so the
  assistant could not change a step it could not see.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.20.0 and 0.19.0 update themselves to 0.20.1 at a
quiet moment, and running workflows go on across the restart.
