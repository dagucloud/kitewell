Kitewell 0.18.3 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- Under every live answer, the chat says what Kitewell is doing and for how
  long: thinking, writing a workflow, trying it, running a step, or waiting
  for you. A long step no longer looks stuck.
- While the assistant writes a workflow, its diagram grows step by step in
  the chat. A trial lights up each step as it runs, then shows where it
  stopped and what each step found as tables, and all of it stays after a
  reload. Rows the assistant's browser reads off a page show as a table too.
- When a workflow depends on what a site, a mailbox, or a file gives, the
  assistant first tries only the steps that read it, so you see real data
  sooner, then adds the rest.
- The assistant now changes its drafts by editing only the lines that
  change, and the chat shows each edit as it lands: the step it changes,
  marked on the diagram, with the lines taken out and put in. Trying a draft
  again after you allow trials, or proposing what was just tried, no longer
  rewrites the whole workflow.
- The chat answers at Medium reasoning effort unless you choose another
  level, so each step takes less time. A level you chose yourself stays as
  it is.
- The questions the assistant asks you also ask whether to allow trial runs,
  so the first trial does not wait on a card of its own.
- Every proposed workflow lists what you asked for, with the step that does
  it and what the trial showed.
- A run started from the chat shows its first report, the values it
  published, and the files it saved on its card once it ends, also after a
  reload.
- Results are no longer written into a workbook that Excel for Mac has
  open. Undo takes back all of a launch's results at once, and they stay out
  of the workbook until you write them again or the row runs again.
- The spreadsheet starters run as their cards say, and a new starter merges
  a folder of branch workbooks into one sheet by column name.
- A kintone connection with OAuth 2.0 sign-in now saves.
- A run's step table labels how long each step took as its duration.
- MCP clients can change a saved workflow by sending edits to its YAML
  instead of the whole YAML, and check those edits first.
- The bundled engine is still Dagu 2.21.0.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.18.2 updates itself to 0.18.3 at a quiet moment, and
running workflows go on across the restart.
