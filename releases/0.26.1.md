Kitewell 0.26.1 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- The chat says what waits for you, such as "A step waits for your reply"
  or "2 changes to decide". Clicking it lists each one with what it asks
  and since when, to open or to set aside as no longer needed. The chat,
  Home, the project list, and the menu bar count the same things, and
  Runs → Waiting lists every wait, however old.
- A project runs where it was made. A computer shows and runs the projects
  made on computers, its own and its team's, which **Projects from your
  team** brings over. Projects made in Kitewell Cloud stay there.
- This computer's connection and plan read the same everywhere. When
  Kitewell Cloud no longer accepts the computer, one message says so and
  until when everything keeps running, and **Sign in again** opens the
  browser at once. A plan granted by contract shows as licensed, with no
  purchase buttons.
- Runs are named by their workflow and start time, with **Copy run ID** in
  the run's menu. A run that waited for approval keeps what started it,
  and a sheet's runs name their sheet and row.
- Schedules read as sentences, such as "On day 1 of every month at 07:00".
- The chat shows what each step did in plain words, with the raw details
  folded away, and finished steps say what they did. Thread titles and
  replies follow the language you write in.
- A paused project explains itself on every page, and **Run now** offers
  to resume it first.
- Japanese reads as Japanese throughout: 2分45秒, lists joined with 、,
  パソコン, and 対応待ち apart from 待機中.
- Errors on the Connect AI apps page and in syncing say what happened and
  what to do, in your language.
- Pages open faster: the workflow and knowledge editors load when first
  opened.
- After an update, projects read "Finishing the update…" while Kitewell
  moves them onto the engine the new version brings. That move and backups
  no longer wait for runs that wait on a person.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.26.0 updates itself to 0.26.1 at a quiet moment,
and running workflows go on across the restart.
