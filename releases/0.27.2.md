Kitewell 0.27.2 is a beta update, with the Dagu 2.27.0 engine.

## What's in this release

- What you type into a box, such as the chore you describe on Home, stays
  while the page refreshes, and a word an input method is still composing
  is not cut short.
- When the account of this computer's chat model has no credits left, the
  chat says so and offers to add credits, to let Kitewell's included model
  answer, or to choose another model. Letting the included model answer
  goes on with what you asked.
- Kitewell's AI model in Settings lists Kitewell (included) on a computer
  signed in to Kitewell, so the chat can go back to it from a model of your
  own.
- Agents & models names the model the chat answers with when this computer
  has set up one of its own.
- On a Mac, it also brings 0.27.1's fix: Kitewell starts when it is opened
  again soon after quitting or after an update.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.27.0 and 0.27.1 update themselves to 0.27.2 at a
quiet moment, and running workflows go on across the restart. From 0.26,
it brings everything in 0.27.0 as well, and messages from earlier chat
threads no longer show; the rest of the conversation stays.
