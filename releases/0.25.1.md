Kitewell 0.25.1 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- On the setup screens, each row's title now leads and its description
  follows in smaller, quieter text. In 0.25.0 the description was drawn
  larger than the title.
- Setup sits in the middle of the window, where on a large window it sat at
  the top.
- Kitewell applies what it builds or changes for you at once, where before
  each workflow, knowledge page, sheet, run, and schedule waited on a card
  for you to apply. The card stays in the chat and shows what was done. To
  decide a kind of change yourself first, turn it on under **Project
  settings**, **Asking before a change**. Sending a secret somewhere in a
  one-off task still asks.
- On a Mac, a chat you have open stays in front while Kitewell works your
  apps, so you can see what it is doing. A docked chat moves to its own
  panel for that time and back afterwards. The steps do not see the chat,
  and clicks pass through it to the app below, so stop the work from the
  menu bar icon.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.25.0 updates itself to 0.25.1 at a quiet moment,
and running workflows go on across the restart.
