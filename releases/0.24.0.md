Kitewell 0.24.0 is a beta update.

## Plans

- Kitewell works without an account for 14 days from the first time it
  opens on a computer, as a trial of Kitewell Team with one project. A
  computer already using Kitewell without an account counts its 14 days
  from the day it updates to this version.
- After those 14 days, **Sign in with Dagu Cloud** starts the workspace's
  own 14-day trial. From three days before a trial ends, the plan banner
  says when it ends and what to do next.
- The free plan now holds no projects or workflows, ahead of the December 1
  date in the 0.22.1 notes. What a plan no longer holds stays to open, read,
  and export. Nothing is deleted.
- Running with nobody signed in now needs Kitewell Team or a trial, and
  Kitewell Personal no longer includes it.

## What's in this release

- You can choose, per workflow, which events reach the AI connected to the
  project and what it should do about each. Each event opens a case the AI
  reads over MCP and closes with a verdict, and a stalled case tells a
  person.
- A workflow can ask for a weekly case that says how its recent runs went:
  their outcomes, how much ran without a model, which steps failed, and how
  earlier cases ended.
- An AI's repair stays apart from the workflow until a trial shows every
  changed step really ran. A step that writes or works the screen is only
  shown in a trial, so that repair goes to a person, and a trial spends no
  model credits. If the next run of the repaired workflow fails, the
  workflow goes back, unless someone has edited it since.
- Whether a repair to a write asks a person is a setting on each workflow
  that only a person changes.
- Kitewell states where the included AI is offered that it keeps nothing
  of what it is sent.
- In Kitewell Cloud, ChatGPT and Claude can reach a project whether or not
  anyone has it open. A workspace that used its Cloud hours is told so, with
  the way to more, and its runs wait. Desktop, coding agent, and Docker
  steps, which a cloud machine cannot run, are refused before they are saved
  and say where to get Kitewell on a computer.
- Someone invited to a team joins from the link at app.kitewell.com and can
  switch workspaces there.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.23.0 updates itself to 0.24.0 at a quiet moment,
and running workflows go on across the restart.
