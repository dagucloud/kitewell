Kitewell 0.27.6 is a beta update, with the Dagu 2.27.0 engine.

## What's in this release

- Google Calendar: workflows can now add, change, and delete events, not
  only read them. A Calendar connection made before offers Update and sign
  in to grant the new access.
- A computer runs at most one browser for every 8 GB of its memory, from
  two to four, and further browser steps wait their turn. A workflow that
  opened many browsers at once could slow every app on the computer,
  Kitewell's own pages included. The chat keeps one fewer open, so a
  workflow's browser never waits for the chat's.
- Switching projects shows the new project at once instead of waiting for
  the page being left to finish loading. Home and Workflows draw before
  the readiness checks answer, and the overview loads faster on a busy
  computer.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell updates itself to 0.27.6 at a quiet moment, and
running workflows go on across the restart. To let workflows change
calendar events, open the Google Calendar connection and choose Update and
sign in.
