Kitewell 0.18.2 is a beta update.

## What's in this release

- Dagu Cloud now chooses the model behind Kitewell's included model, so it
  can move to a newer model without a Kitewell update.
- The included model's page says that your prompts and the model's answers
  are never used for training.
- **Reset the conversation** in the chat header starts the chat over, after
  asking once more, without a trip to device settings.
- Deleting a conversation finishes at once; its trial runs are cleaned up
  afterwards.
- While the engine starts or updates, the project's status says so instead of
  offering **Start engine**.
- Page titles stay readable in a narrow window, as with the chat docked beside
  the pages.
- The bundled engine is still Dagu 2.21.0.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Kitewell 0.18.1 updates itself to 0.18.2 at a quiet moment, and
running workflows go on across the restart.
