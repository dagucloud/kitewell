Kitewell includes Dagu, the workflow engine by the Dagu contributors.
Dagu is distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 3.
The full license is included in DAGU-LICENSE.txt.

Pinned engine release: Dagu 2.22.3
Upstream source: https://github.com/dagucloud/dagu/tree/v2.22.3
Release: https://github.com/dagucloud/dagu/releases/tag/v2.22.3

Go notices, for the standard library and every module Dagu links, are
included in DAGU-GO-LICENSES.txt.

Kitewell's interface and background manager are separate executables that
communicate with Dagu through its command-line and HTTP interfaces.

Go notices, for the standard library and every module the background service
links, including the MCP Go SDK and YAML parser, are included in
GO-LICENSES.txt. Versions are pinned in go.mod and go.sum.

The bundled DAG editor includes syntax and schema editing libraries.
Their notices are included in DAG-EDITOR-LICENSES.txt.

The artifact viewer bundles a Markdown parser and an HTML sanitizer so that
reports written by workflows render without reaching the network.
Their notices are included in MARKDOWN-LICENSES.txt.

Knowledge pages bundle a rich-text editor, its Markdown parser, and the menus
around it, so pages are edited as formatted text without reaching the network.
Their notices are included in KNOWLEDGE-EDITOR-LICENSES.txt.

The Mac app installs its updates with Sparkle 2.10.0, by the Sparkle Project,
which includes bsdiff, sais-lite, and an Ed25519 implementation. Their notices
are included in SPARKLE-LICENSE.txt.

On Windows, the background service reads PDFs with PDFium, the PDF engine of
the Chromium project, carried as WebAssembly, which builds in FreeType,
libjpeg-turbo, OpenJPEG, Little-CMS, zlib, Anti-Grain Geometry, Abseil,
fast_float, and ICU. Their notices are included in PDFIUM-LICENSE.txt.

The Windows app runs on the .NET runtime with WPF and Windows Forms, shows its
window through the Microsoft WebView2 SDK, and sends notifications through the
Windows Community Toolkit. Their notices are included in WINDOWS-LICENSES.txt,
which ships with the Windows app.
